# Data sources (not included in this repository)

- Bengalese finch songs with syllable annotations: Koumura & Okanoya, "BirdsongRecognition" dataset.
- Mouse ultrasonic vocalizations: Goffinet, Brudner, Mooney & Pearson (2021), Duke Research Data Repository, doi:10.7924/r4gq6zn8w (CC0). Folders BM004, BM007, BM010, BM016.
- Dolphin whistles: OpenWhistle, Hugging Face dataset dolphinteam/Whistle-Classification.
- Synthetic repertoires are generated by the scripts in experiments/.
